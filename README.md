# 10 Things I Hate About You

**10 Things I Hate About You** is a 1999 teen romantic comedy based on *The Taming of the Shrew*.

---

## Main Characters

1. Kat Stratford
2. Patrick Verona
3. Bianca Stratford

## Quick Facts

- Released in 1999
- Directed by Gil Junger
- Stars Julia Stiles and Heath Ledger

> Kat's poem is the emotional heart of the film.

Her poem is a `list of things she hates`.
